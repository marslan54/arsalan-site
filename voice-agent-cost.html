Why it is a range. The model and the voice are the swing. Read it as a guide, not a quote. Real calls vary in length, silence is sometimes billed differently, and a model that thinks for longer costs more. Treat this as the right order of magnitude.

Questions

About these numbers.

Why is the advertised per-minute price not what I pay?

Because the headline rate is only the platform's own hosting fee. Vapi advertises $0.05 a minute, and that figure does not include transcription, the language model, voice synthesis or the phone line. Those are billed separately, at cost, and together they are usually worth more than the hosting fee itself.

Why is the answer a range instead of one number?

Because the model and voice you choose move the cost several times over. Vapi publishes the language model at $0.0077 to $0.0452 a minute depending on the model. Picking a single number inside that range would mean inventing one, so this shows both ends and lets you see where you would land.

Who do I pay?

The vendors, directly, on your own accounts. I do not resell minutes or add a margin to them. If I build the agent, you pay me once for the build, and the running cost on this page goes to Vapi and your phone carrier.

Does this include the cost of building the agent?

No. This is the monthly running cost only. The build is a separate one-off cost, and the optional care plan that keeps the agent current is separate again.

Is Vapi the only option?

No. Retell, Bland and ElevenLabs Agents all do the same job with different billing shapes, and some bundle the model and voice into one rate. This calculator uses Vapi because its component pricing is published openly enough to add up in public. The shape of the bill is similar everywhere: a platform fee, a model, a voice, and a phone line.
