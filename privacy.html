Privacy policy.

Plain language, no tracking.

Last updated 16 November 2026

Who I am

This site belongs to Muhammad Arsalan, a freelance AI automation engineer in Karachi, Pakistan. I'm responsible for the information described here. To ask a question or have your information deleted, see Your choices below.

What this site collects

The site itself has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. The only part that stores anything is the AI chat assistant, and only when you use it:

  • Your messages and the assistant's replies.
  • Details you choose to share in the chat, such as your name, email address or WhatsApp number, and what you'd like built.
  • A booking, if you book a call through the chat: your name, email and the time you picked.
  • Technical details: the page you started the chat on, your time zone, and a one-way scrambled code made from your IP address, used only to stop spam. Your actual IP address is not stored.

Your browser keeps a small note (in local storage) so the chat remembers your conversation when you come back. It isn't used for anything else and you can clear it in your browser settings.

Why I use it

  • To answer your questions about my services.
  • To reply to you personally when you ask to talk to me or leave your details.
  • To book a call you asked for.
  • To improve the assistant's answers, for example by noting questions it couldn't answer.

I don't sell your information, use it for advertising, or add you to a mailing list.

Where it goes

These services process chat information on my behalf, only to run the assistant:

  • Supabase stores the conversations, in a data centre in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Render runs the chat service, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Anthropic (Claude) generates the assistant's replies from your messages.
  • Slack delivers me an alert with your question and contact details when you ask to talk to me.
  • Cal.com handles a call booking, if you make one.

The site's text uses fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them, which shows Google your IP address. The pages themselves are hosted on GitHub Pages.

How long I keep it

I keep conversations and contact details for as long as they're useful for following up with you, and delete them when you ask.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete anything you shared in the chat, at any time and for free. Ask the chat assistant to pass your request to me, or message me on Upwork. I'll confirm once it's done.

Please don't share passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in the chat. The assistant never asks for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version goes on this page with a new date at the top.
