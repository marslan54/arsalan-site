Before you book

The practical details.

When I am available

Monday to Friday, 9am to 1pm and 5pm to 10pm Pakistan time (UTC+5). That is the UK morning and the US east coast morning, so most people find a slot without doing any arithmetic. The booking page shows times in your own zone.

How fast I reply

Within one working day, usually the same day. There is no support queue and no triage: messages reach one person, who is also the person who would do the work.

What to have ready

Nothing. If you happen to know roughly how many calls you miss in a week, or how long a new enquiry waits for a reply, the call goes faster. If you do not, we will work it out on the call, which is half of what the call is for.

What I will not do on the call

Ask for card details, send you a proposal you have to chase, or tell you that you need an AI agent when you do not. If forty dollars a month of off-the-shelf software solves your problem, I will name the software.

How to read anything I claim

How I write these pages.

Worth knowing before you weigh anything else on this site, because it tells you which numbers to trust and which claims have runs behind them.

No client names or logos

Most clients sign an NDA. I describe the business and the team, and leave out anything that points to who they are.

A new service says so

Questions

Before you book.

What happens on the call?

Twenty minutes, no slide deck. You describe what is slipping, I ask what a receptionist would ask, and by the end you have either a rough price and timeline or an honest reason not to bother. If a cheap off-the-shelf tool solves it, I will name the tool.

What time zone are you in?

Karachi, UTC+5. Calls are bookable Monday to Friday, 9am to 1pm and 5pm to 10pm Pakistan time, which covers the UK morning and the US east coast morning. The booking page shows the slots in your own time zone.

How quickly do you reply?

Within one working day, and usually the same day. Messages reach one person rather than an inbox, so nothing sits in a queue waiting to be triaged.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. If you happen to know roughly how many calls you miss in a week, or how long it takes someone to reply to a new enquiry, that makes the call faster. If not, we will work it out together.

Can you work with my existing developer or agency?

Yes, and it is often the right shape. I build the agent and hand over the code and the runbook; whoever already looks after your systems keeps looking after them. I am happy to work white-label under an agency's name as well.

Where are you based, and does that matter?

Karachi, Pakistan. Everything is remote, which is normal for this work. It matters in one practical way: your agent runs in your own accounts in your own country, so your data and your phone number never depend on where I happen to live.
