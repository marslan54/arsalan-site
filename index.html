Questions clients ask

What can you automate?

Mostly the work that happens around leads and customers: answering calls, texts and website chats, following up, booking appointments, outbound email, recruiting outreach, and moving data between your tools. If a task repeats and follows rules, it can usually be automated, with a person approving the steps that matter.

Which tools do you work with?

n8n for workflows; Claude, OpenAI and Gemini for the AI; Vapi and Twilio for calls and SMS; the WhatsApp Cloud API; HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Clay, Apollo and Instantly for sales; Cal.com, Slack and Gmail; and TypeScript, Python and Postgres when a project needs real code.

How do you stop an AI agent from going wrong?

The same way I tested banking software. Every agent ships with automated tests, and the conversational ones are put through simulated customers (price pressure, prompt injection, off-topic requests) and graded before launch. They only state facts they were given, never invent prices, and hand over to a person when they should.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the scope: a single automation can take days, a full agent with integrations a few weeks. You get the timeline in the written scope, and a working demo at each milestone along the way.

Where are you, and when can we talk?

I'm in Karachi, Pakistan, on UTC+5 all year. That overlaps with mornings in the US, the full working day in Europe and the Gulf, and afternoons in Australia.

How do we work together and pay?

Through Upwork if you want its contracts and payment protection, hourly or fixed price, or directly once we know each other. Either way it starts with a discovery call and a written scope.
